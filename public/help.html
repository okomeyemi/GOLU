GOLU SUPPORT

How can we help?

Guidance for setting up your account and creating your business website.

How do I create an account?

Choose Sign in with Google. After authentication, complete your profile to open your workspace.

How do I create a website?

Choose Create a website, complete your business brief, select the pages you need and submit your images. GOLU uses this information to generate a custom website specification.

What are reference images?

Reference images communicate visual direction and layout inspiration. They are not copied as finished website designs. Basic allows up to 3 and Pro allows up to 6 per generation.

How do business images work?

Upload real assets such as your logo, products, team photos or gallery images. Give each image a name, description and placement so GOLU can use it in the intended section.

How does AI editing work?

Open a project and describe the change you want in the chat panel. Successful changes consume monthly AI change credits.

Can I publish my website?

The publishing interface is present, but managed deployment is not activated until a deployment provider integration and credentials are configured and verified.

Are payments active?

No. Basic and Pro are paid plan designs, but checkout remains inactive until OPay merchant credentials and webhook processing are configured and tested.
